Why use Marga?

Exam preparation feels lighter when every chapter has a clear status and every revision cycle has a place to land.

Marga makes pending chapters visible so students can stop guessing and start clearing the highest-impact backlog first.

Revision Consistency

Revision dates and confidence labels help you return to weak chapters before they fade from memory.

Syllabus Overwhelm

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for students comparing study trackers, revision planners and exam preparation tools.

Is Marga free?

Yes. Marga is free for students who want to track chapters, syllabus progress and revision.

Can I use Marga offline?

Yes. Marga is built as a lightweight PWA with offline support for saved app assets.

Is Marga useful for JEE preparation?

Yes. JEE aspirants can track Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics chapters, confidence levels, revision dates and syllabus completion.

Does Marga work on mobile devices?

Yes. Marga is responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can ISC students use Marga?

Yes. ISC students can organize Class 11 and Class 12 chapters, monitor backlog and plan revision before boards.

How does Marga help with revision planning?

Marga lets students record revision dates and confidence levels so weak or untouched chapters are easier to identify before exams.
