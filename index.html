Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about Marga’s chapter tracker, revision planning and exam support—so you can decide with confidence.

Marga is a free study tracker built for Indian students preparing for JEE, NEET, ISC and CBSE. It turns your syllabus into chapters you can mark, revise and review on one screen—so you always know what is done, what is pending and what needs another pass before exams.

Yes. Marga is completely free for students. You can track chapters, monitor syllabus completion, plan revisions and view progress without paying for a subscription. Open the app, pick your exam path and start organizing your preparation in minutes.

Yes. Marga runs as a lightweight progressive web app. Once loaded, core study data stays available even when your connection drops—useful during travel, coaching or spotty Wi‑Fi. Install it on your home screen for quick access like a native app.

Yes. Marga is designed mobile-first. The interface works smoothly on phones and tablets, so you can update chapter status, check backlog and plan revision between classes or on the go—without needing a laptop every time.

Add subjects and chapters from your syllabus, then mark each chapter as studied, in progress or not started. Marga surfaces completion percentages and pending work at a glance, so daily study decisions are clear instead of buried in notebooks or chat groups.

Marga supports JEE (Main and Advanced), NEET, ISC (Class 11 and 12) and CBSE board preparation. Each path is organized by subject and chapter, so you can focus on the syllabus structure that matches your exam—not a generic to-do list.

Yes. Record when you last revised a chapter and set confidence levels to flag weak or untouched topics. Marga makes gaps visible early, so revision cycles stay planned instead of rushed in the final weeks before mocks or boards.

You can explore Marga without an account, but signing in syncs your progress securely across devices. That way your chapter tracker, revision notes and syllabus view stay intact when you switch from phone to desktop or return after a break.

Yes. Marga supports custom exams, allowing you to create subjects and chapters for any syllabus, so students can organize preparation for any exam they choose.
